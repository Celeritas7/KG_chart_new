## Grammar notes from the lesson
- **Three meanings of "keep":** வைத்துக்கொள் (hold on to), வைத்திரு / இரு (stay the same), தொடர்ந்து செய் (continue).
- **Open vs closed:** say "Keep the door open", with no -ed, but "Keep the door closed", with -ed.
- **Command vs keep:** "Open the door" (திற) is a one-time action. "Keep the door open" (திறந்தே வை) means don't close it. The same goes for "Clean the room" vs "Keep the room clean".
- **"Keep" + verb always takes -ing:** "Keep try" is wrong; say "Keep trying".
- **Smiling vs laughing:** "Keep smiling" is about a gentle smile. "Keep laughing" means laughing out loud.
- **Quiet vs quite:** quiet means அமைதி; quite means "very" / "fairly".
